# AI-assisted work disclosure

Waybill was primarily designed and developed by our team. We used AI tools as
an additional development aid during parts of the project, especially when we
needed a second perspective or wanted to move more quickly through routine
implementation work.

## Where AI helped

AI assistance was used for some early scaffolding, API and database drafts,
debugging suggestions, test-case ideas, deployment troubleshooting and the
initial structure of parts of the documentation.

It was also useful for explaining errors and suggesting ways to compare the
implementation against the challenge requirements.

## What the team created and decided

The team made the product decisions and remained responsible for the final
result, including:

- interpreting the challenge and Designathon requirements;
- defining the dispatcher, loader, driver and store-manager workflows;
- choosing the operating constraints and degradation scenarios;
- designing the visual direction and responsive role experiences;
- implementing and integrating the application;
- reviewing the generated suggestions and changing them where needed;
- testing the role flows, edge cases and deployment;
- preparing the walkthrough, demo and final submission.

The final code and documentation were reviewed by the team. We ran the
available automated checks and manually tested the main role flows before
submission. AI tools did not make the final product decisions or replace the
team's review and testing.
