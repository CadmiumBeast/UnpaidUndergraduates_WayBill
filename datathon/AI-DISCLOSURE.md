# Datathon AI-tool disclosure

The team used AI assistance as a development aid while preparing this
submission. 

## AI-assisted work

AI assistance helped with:

- organizing the repository and submission checklist;
- suggesting baseline modelling approaches and leakage checks;
- debugging Python and CSV schema issues.

## Team-owned work and review

The team chose the features, labels, chronological split, Task 2A forecast
formula, Task 2B prioritization policy and feasibility constraints. The team
reviewed and edited the implementation, regenerated the artifacts locally and
checked the outputs against the supplied templates and allocation validator.

No proprietary API was used to generate predictions, no pre-trained model was
used, and the competition data was not uploaded or published. =
